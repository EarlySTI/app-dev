# My Favorite Series: Adventure Time

**Adventure Time** is a Cartoon Network series about *Finn* and *Jake*, best friends who go on adventures in the Land of Ooo.

![Adventure Time](adventure-time.jpg)

---

## Wholesome Episode: "I Remember You"

### What it's about

Marceline visits the Ice King, and they spend time together. We learn that before he became the Ice King, he was a kind man named Simon who took care of Marceline when she was young.

> A wholesome episode doesn't need a big battle, just a lot of heart.

### Why I like it

- The friendship between Marceline and the Ice King feels real
- The music is beautiful
- It shows the gentle side of a "villain"

### My top 3 reasons to watch it

1. It's emotional but still sweet
2. The characters have a deep backstory
3. It teaches that caring for someone matters

### Episode details

- Series name: `Adventure Time`
- Episode name: `I Remember You`
